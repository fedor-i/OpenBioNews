A “Source” link won’t open? (403 / redirect)

Every card links to the real government record. When a link is blocked, it’s the destination’s bot-protection (Akamai/CloudFront) refusing your browser — not this site. Common culprits and fixes:

  • Brave Shields (or hardened privacy browsers): tap the 🦁 and lower Shields for that site — its anti-fingerprinting trips the WAF.
  • Ad/tracker/privacy extensions (uBlock strict, Ghostery, Privacy Badger, AdGuard, DuckDuckGo): pause them for the government site.
  • VPN, Tor, or a datacenter/proxy IP: gov WAFs flag these — turn it off or switch to a residential connection.
  • In-app browsers (opening from Instagram, X, LinkedIn, Slack): use “Open in Safari/Chrome” instead of the app’s built-in viewer.
  • Blocked cookies/JavaScript or a strict content filter (school, hospital or corporate network, e.g. Zscaler): the WAF sets a cookie the page needs — allow cookies/JS or try another network.

Plain Safari, Chrome, Edge or Firefox with no blockers open every source normally.
